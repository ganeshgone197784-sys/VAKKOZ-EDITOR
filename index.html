What I can edit for you

From a 20-second reel to a full cinematic film, each edit is shaped around your story and your platform.

Short-Form Reels

Vertical edits for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok. Tight cuts, beat-matched timing, captions and framing that fits the screen.

YouTube Video Editing

Clear, watchable long-form edits with good pacing, cutaways, titles and balanced sound, cut to keep viewers watching.

Cinematic Films

Story-first edits with a considered rhythm, music sync and a consistent visual tone, for short films, travel stories and personal projects.

Wedding & Event Highlights

Highlight films that keep the feeling of the day: key moments, speeches and music, shaped into one short story you can rewatch.

Brand & Promotional Videos

Product and brand videos cut around one clear message, using your colors, logo and call to action.

Color Grading & Motion Graphics

Color correction and creative looks, plus animated titles, lower thirds and simple motion graphics for a finished feel.

Why work with me

These are the values behind every edit. They describe how I approach the work, so you know what to expect.

  • Storytelling first

    Every edit starts with what the viewer should feel. Pacing and structure follow from that.

  • Attention to detail

    Clean cuts, balanced audio and steady framing from the first second to the last.

  • Smooth transitions

    Movement that carries the story forward instead of pulling attention away from it.

  • Color grading

    A consistent look that suits the mood of your footage and the platform it will live on.

  • Platform-ready exports

    The right ratio, resolution and file size for Reels, YouTube or a big screen.

  • Clear communication

    You know what happens next at every stage, and your feedback is always welcome.

How we’ll work together

Four simple steps from first message to final file.

  1. 01

    Brief & Ideas

    You share your footage, references and goals. We agree on length, platform, style and deadline before editing starts.

  2. 02

    Editing & Storytelling

    The first cut is built around the story: structure, pacing, music, sound and transitions.

  3. 03

    Review & Revisions

    You watch the draft and send your thoughts. The edit is refined until it feels right.

  4. 04

    Final Delivery

    Your finished video is exported in the right format and resolution for where it will be posted.
